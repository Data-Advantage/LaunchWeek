## What Is LaunchWeek?

LaunchWeek is a free, open-source playbook for indie founders who have built a product and now need to launch it. 33 action-oriented guides covering the complete GTM journey — from market research to public launch — in 5 days.

## The 5-Day Playbook

| Day | Theme | What You Build |
|-----|-------|----------------|
| [Day 0: Research](content/0-research/README.md) | Understand your market | Market brief, competitive matrix, ICP, positioning, demand validation |
| [Day 1: Position](content/1-position/README.md) | Nail your story | Value prop, pricing, landing page copy, brand voice, pitch deck |
| [Day 2: Content](content/2-content/README.md) | Build your content engine | SEO strategy, 5 blog posts, social calendar, email sequences, AEO/GEO |
| [Day 3: Distribute](content/3-distribute/README.md) | Get in front of your ICP | Social profiles, community presence, cold outreach, partnerships, GitHub |
| [Day 4: Convert](content/4-convert/README.md) | Turn visitors into customers | Analytics, A/B testing, onboarding, pricing page, Stripe, churn |
| [Day 5: Launch](content/5-launch/README.md) | Execute a coordinated launch | Launch checklist, community launch, HN, press, announcements, retrospective |

## 33 Guides. All Free.

Every guide is:
- **Action-oriented** — prompts, templates, and checklists you can use today
- **AI-accelerated** — specific prompts for Claude, Perplexity, and FastWrite
- **Founder-to-founder** — direct, no fluff, written for people who are building
