# Day 2: Content

*Content is your growth engine. Build the SEO foundation and create the first 30 days of content.*

## What Today Covers

Day 2 is where you build the content infrastructure that compounds over time. SEO content takes months to rank — which means the best time to start was 6 months ago, and the second best time is today.

By the end of Day 2, you have:
- A keyword strategy that targets what your ICP actually searches for
- 5 published blog posts driving search traffic
- A 30-day social content calendar
- Email sequences ready to send
- A system for turning one piece of content into ten

The goal is not to produce content for the sake of content. Every piece you create today should have a clear answer to: "Why will the right person find this, and what will they do next?"

**This day typically takes 6-8 hours — the heaviest content day of the week.**
