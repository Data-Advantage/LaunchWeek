# Day 5: Launch

*This is what the week was for. Execute a coordinated launch and capture every signal.*

## What Today Covers

Launch day is not the end of LaunchWeek — it's the beginning of your distribution flywheel. Today you execute the plan you've been building, show up in the right places, and create the social momentum that carries your product into the next phase.

The founder mistake is treating launch as a one-day event. The best launches are coordinated across multiple channels over 48-72 hours. You want multiple "launch moments," not one big bang that fizzles.

**Launch day is high-intensity and requires full attention. Clear your calendar.**
