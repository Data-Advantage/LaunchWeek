# Day 0: Research

*Before you launch anything, understand who you're launching to and what you're up against.*

## What Today Covers

You built something. Now comes the part most founders skip: understanding the market before spending a dollar on marketing. Day 0 is about building your intelligence foundation — the market context, competitive landscape, and customer insight that everything else depends on.

Skip this day and you'll write landing page copy for the wrong audience, pick keywords no one searches, and position against competitors you don't understand. Do it right and every subsequent day gets easier and more effective.

**This day typically takes 4-6 hours. It's the highest-leverage work you'll do all week.**

## What You'll Have at the End of Day 0

- A one-page market brief documenting your opportunity
- A competitive landscape map showing exactly how you differ from alternatives
- 3 specific, validated customer personas
- A defensible positioning statement
- Evidence of real demand (or a pivot signal)

## Tools You'll Use

- **Perplexity** — live web search + research synthesis
- **Claude** — structured analysis and framework application
- **ChatGPT Deep Research** — multi-source research reports
- **[LLMReference.com](https://llmreference.com)** — compare AI models for research tasks
- **Reddit / Twitter / Indie Hackers** — community signal and validation
