# Product Strategy: LaunchWeek

## Vision

"Launch Your SaaS in Just One Week." A product launch framework and toolkit that gives founders a structured, day-by-day plan to go from idea to shipped product in 7 days. Content site with guides, templates, and resources.

## Target User

Solo founders, indie hackers, and small teams who want to ship fast. They have an idea and need a structured framework to get to launch without overthinking.

## Positioning

The definitive launch playbook for AI-era founders. Step-by-step framework, not theory. Actionable daily guides with templates, checklists, and example projects.

## Current Stage

Content site live at launchweek.ai. All guides, templates, and demo repo shipped. SEO-first distribution.

## Current Priorities

1. SEO growth — rank for "launch SaaS in a week," "SaaS launch checklist," related queries
2. Content depth — expand guides based on search demand
3. Lead generation — capture email signups for newsletter/course upsell

## Key Decisions

- **Content-only product**: No SaaS features. Value is in the framework and guides.
- **Stack**: Next.js, static content. No Convex or Clerk.
- **Tier 2 social**: SEO-first. Share via Data Advantage hub.
- **Companion to VibeWeek**: LaunchWeek = business framework, VibeWeek = technical build challenge.
